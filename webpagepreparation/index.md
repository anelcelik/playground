# Playground Tracker

Keep track of your family's playground time, together.

Playground Tracker is a simple log for parents. Record who took the kids out,
for how long and what they did, see how the week went at a glance, and share
the log with your partner through iCloud.

[Download on the App Store]({{APP_STORE_LINK}})

## What it does

- **Log a visit in seconds.** Tap Morning or Evening, or repeat your usual
  visit with one tap.
- **Fill in a day you missed.** Tap any past day in the week strip, or pick an
  older date.
- **Days nobody went count too.** Mark them, pick a reason or write your own,
  and see the reasons add up in History.
- **Plans.** Set up activities that repeat on fixed days, or a one-time event,
  with an optional reminder. Each plan keeps its own history: done, skipped or
  missed.
- **Add plans to your calendar.** Open the plan and add it to your iPhone
  calendar, then invite people from there.
- **Share with your family.** Invite your partner with a link; both phones stay
  in sync through iCloud.
- **Your look.** Choose between the Modernist and Swing color themes, light or
  dark, and 12- or 24-hour time.

## What it doesn't do

- No account to create.
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- No subscription: you pay once ({{PRICE}}).
- No servers of ours: your log stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

## Requirements

iPhone with iOS 15 or later. It also runs on iPad as an iPhone app.
