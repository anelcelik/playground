## Description

Playground Tracker is a simple log for parents who want to know how much time the kids really spend outside, and who took them.

LOG A VISIT IN SECONDS
Tap Morning or Evening, choose who went, which kids, how long and what they did. Once you have a routine, "Same as usual" repeats your last visit with one tap.

FILL IN THE DAYS YOU MISSED
Tap any past day in the week strip, or pick an older date, and log or change it like today.

DAYS NOBODY WENT COUNT TOO
Mark them with a reason, such as rain, sick or busy, or write your own. When only one parent went, note why the other couldn't. History shows the reasons add up over time.

PLANS
Set up activities that repeat on fixed days, or a one-time event, with an optional reminder. Mark each one done or skipped. Every plan keeps its own history, so you can see how often it really happened.

ADD PLANS TO YOUR CALENDAR
Open a plan and add it to your iPhone calendar, repeat included, then invite people from there.

SHARE WITH YOUR FAMILY
Invite your partner with a link. Your log stays in sync on both phones through iCloud.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose the Modernist or Swing color theme, light or dark mode, and 12- or 24-hour time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. No analytics. Your log stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and we never see it.

One purchase, no subscription.

## Review notes (App Review Information > Notes)

Playground Tracker works fully offline; no account or sign-in is needed.

To try it: open Today, tap Morning, pick a parent and tap Save visit. Tap a
past day in the week strip to log or edit that day. "Nobody went today"
records a day without a visit.

Plans: open the Plans tab, tap +, create a plan; it appears on Today with
Done / Skip. Tap the plan in Plans to see its history and "Add to Calendar",
which opens the system New Event sheet (write-only calendar access).

Family sync (Settings > Family sync) uses CloudKit sharing and needs a second
Apple ID to accept the invitation; everything else in the app works without
it.

Notifications are only requested when a reminder is switched on (Settings >
Reminders, or the reminder switch in a plan).
