# Playground Tracker Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Please include your
iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I log a visit?

Open **Today**, tap **Morning** or **Evening**, choose who went, which kids, how
long and what they did, then tap **Save visit**. Once you have logged a visit,
**Same as usual** repeats your last one with a single tap.

### Can I log or change a day in the past?

Yes. Tap a day in the week strip at the top of **Today**. For an older day, tap
the date above the title and pick it from the calendar. **TODAY** brings you
back.

### Nobody went today. How do I record that?

Tap **Nobody went today** and pick a reason, or tap **+ Write your own**. For a
holiday, tap **On vacation**. Reasons you write are remembered for next time.

### Only one parent went. Can I note why the other didn't?

Yes. When only some parents are ticked in the visit sheet, the app asks why the
others didn't go. Answering is optional.

### How do plans work?

Open **Plans** and tap **+**. Choose **Repeats** for activities on fixed days
(for example football every Monday and Wednesday), or **One time** for a single
date. Plans show up on **Today**, where you mark them **Done** or **Skip**. Tap
a plan in **Plans** to see how often it actually happened.

### How do I add a plan to my calendar and invite people?

Open **Plans**, tap the plan, then tap **Add to Calendar**. Your iPhone's own
New Event screen opens with the plan filled in. Add people under **Invitees**
and tap **Add**; your calendar sends the invitations.

### How do I share the log with my partner?

Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud. Go to **Settings > Family sync**,
tap **Invite someone** and send the link. When your partner taps the link and
accepts, both phones stay in sync automatically.

### My reminders don't arrive.

Check **Settings > Reminders** in the app, then make sure notifications are
allowed in iOS: **Settings > Notifications > Playground Tracker**.

### How do I change the colors, dark mode or the time format?

**Settings > Date, time & theme** in the app.

### Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you are signed in. We cannot see it.
See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy) for details.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete entries in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes everything on
the iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then
iCloud, open the storage list, choose Playground Tracker and delete its data.

### Does it work on iPad?

It runs on iPad as an iPhone app.

### Is there a subscription?

No. Playground Tracker is a one-time purchase, with no in-app purchases and no
ads.
