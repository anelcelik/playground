# Playground Tracker Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Please include your
iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I log a visit?

Open **Today**, tap **Morning** or **Evening**, choose who went, which kids, how
long and what they did, then tap **Save visit**. Once you have logged a visit,
**Same as usual** repeats your last one with a single tap.

### Can I log or change a day in the past?

Yes. Tap a day in the week strip at the top of **Today**. For an older day, tap
the date above the title and pick it from the calendar. **TODAY** brings you
back.

### Where do I see everything I logged?

Open **History** and tap the list icon at the top: every visit, vacation and
nobody-went day, newest first. Tap one to edit or delete it. On **Today**, long-press a logged
**Morning** or **Evening** box to edit or delete that visit.

### Nobody went today. How do I record that?

Tap **Nobody went today** and pick a reason, or tap **+ Write your own**. For a
holiday, tap **On vacation**. Reasons you write are remembered for next time.

### Only one parent went. Can I note why the other didn't?

Yes. When only some parents are ticked in the visit sheet, the app asks why the
others didn't go. Answering is optional.

### How do plans work?

Open **Plans** and tap **+**. Choose **Repeats** for activities on fixed days
(for example football every Monday and Wednesday), or **One time** for a single
date. Plans show up on **Today**, where you mark them **Done** or **Skip**. Tap
a plan in **Plans** to see how often it actually happened.

### How do I add a plan to my calendar and invite people?

When you create a plan, pick **Calendar** under **Also add to**. Later, open
**Plans**, tap the plan, then tap **Add to Calendar**. Your iPhone's own New
Event screen opens with the plan filled in. Add people under **Invitees** and
tap **Add**; your calendar sends the invitations.

### Can a plan go into the Reminders app instead?

Yes. Pick **Reminders** under **Also add to** when you create the plan, or tap
**Add to Reminders** on the plan's page. It becomes a reminder with an alert at
the plan's time, repeating on the plan's days.

### How do I share the log with my partner?

Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud. Go to **Settings > Family sync**,
tap **Invite someone** and send the link. When your partner taps the link and
accepts, both phones stay in sync automatically, and your partner's app takes
over the names you set up.

### What exactly is shared?

Visits, vacation and nobody-went days, the names of parents and kids, and the
activities and reasons you typed. Plans are shared too, with the days you
marked **Done** or **Skip**. Reminders (also a plan's own reminder), the outdoor
goal and the look of the app are set on each phone.

### Can I get my log out of the app?

Yes. **Settings > Export** makes a CSV file of every entry and opens the share
sheet, so you can save it to Files or send it to yourself. It opens in Numbers
and Excel.

### I renamed someone. What happens to the old visits?

They are renamed too. Change the name in **Settings > Parents and kids**; the
visits and plans under the old name follow, and their counts stay together.

### Why didn't I get a reminder today?

The daily reminders stay quiet on a day that is already logged.

### My reminders don't arrive.

Check **Settings > Reminders** in the app, then make sure notifications are
allowed in iOS: **Settings > Notifications > Playground Tracker**.

### How do I change the language, colors or time format?

**Settings > Language, date & theme** in the app. The app speaks English,
Spanish, German, Bosnian and Czech. It follows your iPhone's language until you
pick one, and the very first screen of the app lets you choose too.

### Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you are signed in. We cannot see it.
See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy) for details.

### How do I delete my data?

Go to **Settings > Delete all data**. It removes everything from your iPhone
and from your iCloud at once. If you share the log with family, sharing stops
and the shared copy is deleted; what is already on their iPhones stays until
they use **Delete all data** there too. You can also delete single entries at
any time.

### Does it work on iPad?

Playground Tracker is made for iPhone. There is no iPad version.

### Is there a subscription?

No. Playground Tracker is a one-time purchase, with no in-app purchases and no
ads.
